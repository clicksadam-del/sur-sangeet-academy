About
Sur Sangeet Academy

Sur Sangeet Academy is an online learning platform based in Rajajipuram, Lucknow, dedicated to bringing the beauty of Indian dance and music to learners everywhere.

Our aim is to make traditional arts accessible while creating a joyful and disciplined learning environment.
