The Story of Kathak

Kathak is one of the eight classical dance forms of India, rooted in the storytelling tradition of North Indian bards. It is known for its intricate footwork, rapid spins called chakkars, and expressive hand gestures and facial expression, known as abhinaya, used to narrate stories through movement.

The Grace of Semi Classical

Semi Classical dance blends the technique and grace of classical Indian dance with more lyrical, contemporary movement. It offers students a graceful middle ground between strict classical form and expressive, modern choreography.

A Kathak-Bollywood Fusion

Bollywood Kathak is a fusion style that combines the precision of classical Kathak footwork and spins with the energy, music, and choreography style popularized by Hindi cinema, making it a lively option for students who enjoy both traditions.

The Energy of Bollywood Dance

Bollywood dance draws on a mix of classical, folk, and contemporary Indian dance styles set to popular Hindi film music. It is known for its high energy, expressive choreography, and wide appeal across age groups.
